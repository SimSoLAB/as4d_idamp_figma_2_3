DEFECT

Local damage and missing material define the repair input.

CUT-OFF

Damaged material is removed to a controlled repair boundary.

REBUILD

Material is restored where geometry and function are required.

INTEGRATE

The functional damping geometry becomes part of the repaired component.

EMBEDDED

VERIFY
